VITUE · HABIT TRACKER

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Vitue Habit Tracker account and its associated data.

If you can still use the app

  1. Open Vitue Habit Tracker and go to Profile.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Review the warning and type DELETE to confirm.

The in-app process removes your Firebase Authentication account and the Firestore user document with its nested habits, check-ins, program enrollments, and program activities. It also clears the app's local Room data on that device. A minimal UID-only deletion-prevention marker may be retained temporarily so an old signed-in session cannot recreate deleted records.

If you cannot access the app

Send an account-deletion request from the email address associated with your Vitue account. Do not include your password or verification codes.

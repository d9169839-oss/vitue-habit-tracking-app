Overview & Privacy Commitments

Virtue Habit Tracker ("Virtue", "we", "our", or "the App") is a daily habit-building, physical fitness, and behavioral wellness application for Android. We believe your personal growth and health journey belong exclusively to you.

Zero Ads • Zero Data Selling • On-Device Biometrics: Virtue Habit Tracker contains no third-party advertising SDKs and never sells, rents, or shares your personal or health data with advertisers or data brokers. Biometric calibration inputs (sex, age, height, weight) are stored strictly locally on your device to compute BMR/TDEE targets and are never uploaded to cloud servers.

2. Google Play Health Policy & Medical / Psychological Disclaimer

Not a Medical Device or Clinical Healthcare Service: Virtue Habit Tracker is a general self-improvement, fitness, and habit-tracking application. It is NOT a medical device, clinical diagnostic instrument, or licensed psychotherapy provider, and has not been evaluated by the FDA or any medical regulatory agency.
  • Fitness & Metabolic Calculations (BMR / TDEE): Caloric estimates and training volume targets are mathematical approximations calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for educational purposes only. Always consult a physician before beginning any new exercise or nutrition regimen.
  • Behavioral Wellness, CBT-Inspired & Sleep Hygiene Programs: Our mindfulness, stress reduction, sleep hygiene (CBT-I principles), and focus curricula are for general self-help wellness only and do not diagnose, treat, or replace professional psychiatric or psychological care.
Emergency Notice: If you are experiencing a medical emergency or mental health crisis, stop using the App immediately and contact your local emergency services (e.g., 911 or 988 in the US/Canada, 112 in Europe).

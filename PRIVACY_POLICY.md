# Privacy Policy — Virtue Habit Tracker

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Application ID:** `com.ytron.virtuhabit`

Virtue Habit Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") helps you build consistent daily habits and follow personalized growth and fitness programs. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, how it is protected, and how you can delete your account and data at any time.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Account & Profile Information
- **Email address and Display Name:** Collected when you register with email/password or sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication.
- **User Identifier (UID):** An internal authentication identifier used to isolate and sync your personal data across your devices.

### b. Habit & Program Data
- **Habits and Daily Check-Ins:** Habit titles, descriptions, creation dates, archive status, and daily completion records.
- **Program Enrollments & Activities:** Selected program templates, schedule preferences (available minutes/day, days/week, experience level, work activity level, equipment access), and daily activity completion status.
- **Biometric Calibration Inputs (In-Memory Only):** When personalizing a fitness program, you may enter biological sex, age, height, and weight to compute estimated BMR/TDEE and training volume. These inputs are processed on-device during plan calibration and are not stored in cloud databases.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:
1. Authenticate your account and keep your session secure.
2. Store your habits, check-in streaks, and active programs locally on your device (using Room SQLite) and synchronize them with your private cloud workspace (Google Cloud Firestore).
3. Deliver optional local daily habit reminder notifications on your device.

We **do not** sell, rent, or share your personal or health/fitness data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **In Transit:** All network communication between the App and Firebase Authentication / Cloud Firestore is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
- **Access Control:** Cloud Firestore security rules strictly enforce that only your authenticated account (`request.auth.uid`) can read, write, or delete your personal documents.
- **On Device:** Habit and program records are cached in the application's private sandboxed database and cleared automatically when you sign out or delete your account.

---

## 4. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your account and data:

### In-App Deletion (Immediate)
1. Open the **Virtue Habit Tracker** app and sign in.
2. Navigate to the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Tap **Delete Account Permanently** and confirm.
4. This immediately deletes your cloud documents (`habits`, `habitCheckIns`, `programEnrollments`, `programActivities`, and user profile) from Cloud Firestore, deletes your Firebase Authentication account, and wipes the local device database.

### Web / Email Deletion Request
If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, you can request full account and data deletion via our [Account Deletion Request Guide](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md).
